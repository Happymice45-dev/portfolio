Sir Syed Ahmad Khan

1817 - 1898 | Educator, Reformer, and Scholar

If you have time, you should read more about this incredible human being on his Wikipedia entry.

Timeline and Legacy of Sir Syed Ahmad Khan

  • 1817: Born in Delhi, Mughal Empire.
  • 1838: Joined the service of the East India Company and became a respected judge.
  • 1859: Established modern schools in Muradabad and Ghazipur to promote modern education.
  • 1875: Founded the Muhammadan Anglo-Oriental (MAO) College in Aligarh to combine modern scientific education with Islamic values.
  • 1886: Established the All India Muhammadan Educational Conference to promote educational awareness across the subcontinent.
  • 1898: Passed away in Aligarh, leaving behind a lasting legacy of educational empowerment.
"Acquisition of knowledge of science and technology is the only way for the progress of the nation."
— Sir Syed Ahmad Khan
